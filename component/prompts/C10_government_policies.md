## Prompt

You are helping me write a section of an academic survey on "Security and Safety of AI-Generated Content." This section (Section 4.1) covers government policies and international cooperation on AIGC governance. Focus on institutions, legislation, and standards — no technical implementation details.

Please produce a comprehensive research document covering:

**1. EUROPEAN UNION**

- EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689):
  - Risk-tiered framework: unacceptable / high / limited / minimal risk — explain each tier with examples relevant to AIGC.
  - Article 50 transparency obligations — specifically the deepfake labeling requirements effective August 2, 2025. What exactly must be labeled and how?
  - Penalties: up to €35M or 7% of global annual revenue.
  - Implementation timeline: which provisions are already in force, which are upcoming.
  - AI Office establishment and its role.
- Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content (first draft December 2025):
  - Multi-layer marking approach: metadata + implicit watermarks + visible labels.
  - The interim "AI" icon proposal.
  - Participating organizations and compliance expectations.
- Digital Services Act (DSA): platform obligations specifically for AI-generated content — what do platforms need to do?
- GDPR implications for AI training data: right to erasure vs. model training, data protection impact assessments for generative AI.

**2. UNITED STATES**

- Federal level:
  - Executive Order 14110 on Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy AI (October 2023) — key provisions relevant to AIGC.
  - Subsequent policy adjustments under the Trump administration (2025–2026) — document any executive orders revoking or modifying Biden-era AI policy.
  - NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) and its Generative AI Profile.
- State level:
  - California: SB 1047 (or successor bills), deepfake laws, AB 2655 (election deepfakes).
  - Texas: deepfake-related legislation.
  - New York: relevant AI and deepfake laws.
  - Other notable state-level actions.
- Congressional progress: overview of key bills (e.g., DEFIANCE Act, No AI FRAUD Act, REAL Political Ads Act) and their status.

**3. CHINA**

- Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services (effective August 15, 2023): key requirements — content review, training data compliance, user identity verification.
- Deep Synthesis Regulations (effective January 10, 2023): labeling requirements, identity verification, content management.
- Algorithm Filing System: how it works, scope, and enforcement.
- TC260 "Basic Security Requirements for Generative AI Services" (draft standard): the 5 categories and 31 security risks framework. What does compliance require?
- Practical enforcement: any documented enforcement actions.

**4. INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION**

- UK AI Safety Institute → renamed AI Security Institute: mandate, key outputs, evaluation work.
- International AI Safety Reports: 2025 edition (Bengio et al.) and 2026 edition — key findings and recommendations relevant to AIGC.
- AI Safety Summit series: Bletchley Park (Nov 2023) → Seoul (May 2024) → Paris (Feb 2025) → India (2026 planned) — key outcomes and declarations from each.
- G7 Hiroshima AI Process and International Code of Conduct for Organizations Developing Advanced AI Systems.
- OECD AI Principles and their updates.
- Comparative analysis paragraph: EU (prescriptive, rule-based) vs. US (voluntary, principle-based) vs. China (application-specific, centrally administered) — strengths and weaknesses of each approach.

Provide complete references — full legislation titles with article/section numbers, official document URLs, government publication references, dates of enactment/effectiveness. This section requires authoritative primary sources.
